Small pill label for brand phrases, categories, and the "turista vs vecino" chips.

```jsx
<Badge tone="rojo" solid>Bienvenido a la terraza</Badge>
<Badge tone="sea">Liv</Badge>
<Badge tone="firma">Palabras con Alma</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `neutral` · `rojo` · `sun` · `sea` · `firma` (bougainvillea).
- `solid` fills with the colour and uses white text; default is a soft tint.
