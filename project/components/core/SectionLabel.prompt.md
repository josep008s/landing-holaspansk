Uppercase, widely-tracked overline used to title a section. Pair with a serif headline below.

```jsx
<SectionLabel tone="naranja" divider>Palabras con Alma</SectionLabel>
```

- `tone`: `naranja` (default) · `rojo` · `sea` · `soft`.
- `divider` adds the 60px rojo→amarillo gradient bar. `align` left or center.
