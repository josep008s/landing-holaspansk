Warm surface card with an optional painted-image header. The image zooms gently and the card lifts on hover. Use for editorial / story cards — never a hard grey box.

```jsx
<Card image="assets/img/sorolla-pergola.jpg" eyebrow="Kultur" title="La sobremesa">
  Den lange stunden etter maten der ingen reiser seg.
</Card>
```

- `image` + `imageAlt` add a 16:10 header. `eyebrow` is the tracked uppercase label (Naranja); `title` renders in Fraunces.
- `hoverable` (default true) lifts the card and zooms the image.
