Pill-shaped brand button — warm and confident, the only CTA shape holaSpansk uses. Reach for it on every call-to-action.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" arrow>¿Vienes?</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Les mer</Button>
<Button as="a" href="#nyhetsbrev" variant="ghost" arrow>Nos vemos en la terraza</Button>
```

- `variant`: `primary` (Rojo, default), `sun` (Amarillo, dark text), `secondary` (outline), `ghost`.
- `size`: `sm` · `md` · `lg`. `arrow` appends the brand → glyph.
- Hover lifts (scale 1.04) and, on primary, deepens to Rojo-oscuro with a rojo glow. Always pill radius — never square a CTA.
