The flagship "Palabras con Alma" unit — an untranslatable Spanish word set huge in Fraunces italic on the sun-warm gradient, over the dark ink surface. The cornerstone of the brand's content.

```jsx
<PalabraCard
  word="sobremesa"
  meaning="Den lange stunden etter maten der ingen reiser seg."
  bridge="Det finnes ikke ett norsk ord for det."
  close="Tid uten klokke."
/>
```

- `word` is required-feeling; `meaning` (the Spanish/emotional jewel), `bridge` (Norwegian explanation) and `close` (poetic line) are optional layers.
- Keep the poetic close short — "if you explain the magic, you kill it."
